# Sound Pack SFX — Catalog & Usage Guide

Location: `tools/Sound pack SFX/` — 188 files in 4 folders.
Note: lengths below are measured with `ffprobe` (seconds, ≈1 decimal). Character
notes are from filenames — verify tone with a quick listen before final use.

## Mixing guidelines
- Ambience beds: −30…−24 dB; whoosh on cuts: −18…−12 dB; UI pops: −14…−10 dB; impacts: −10…−6 dB.
- Duck ambience/music under dialogue (TTS voice is king — never compete with it).
- One accent sound per cut; don't stack whoosh + impact + riser on the same moment
  (riser → impact is a pair, whoosh is for the cut itself).
- Prefer dry/short variants for small foley; keep reverb-heavy ones for card/full-frame moments.
- Fade ambience in/out over 0.5–1 s; never hard-cut a bed.
- Beds < 60 s must be looped (with a short crossfade) to cover a full scene; the
  four >60 s beds cover most scenes in a single unedited pass.
- **Always measure the source file's own loudness (`ffmpeg volumedetect`) before setting
  `gain`** — some files are recorded very quiet (e.g. the conversation murmur: mean −44 dB
  needs gain ≈ 1.4, while a normal −25 dB file needs ≈ 0.1). Judge the final bed by its
  level in the dialogue gaps of the mix (~−27 dB is a good target), not by the gain number.

- Engine: `tools/assemble.js` reads `episodes/<id>/sound.json` (`beds`/`hits`/`intro`/
  `outro`, `loop` support via `-stream_loop`). All segments forced stereo (`-ac 2`) —
  mono/stereo mismatch across concat segments breaks audio playback.

## Where this fits the pipeline
See `docs/EPISODE-PLAYBOOK.md` — sounds are consumed at **render/assembly time**
(`tools/assemble.js` stage): ambience beds under scenes, whooshes on shot cuts and
card pops, UI tones on hook/explosion cards, foley synced to on-screen actions.
If possible, add them during scene construction (timeline flags per line); otherwise
apply them in the final edit pass over `episodes/<id>/render/final.mp4`.
